Wheel Canvas

One on-chain canvas per day of Squeektoshi on the wheel. Each dot is one 10 minute round; bigger and brighter means more work. Painted from SqueekCore round data on Ethereum Sepolia.

Unofficial community project. Testnet only, nothing here has real value.

The first mint of a day reads that day from SqueekCore and stores it in the contract, so a canvas never changes afterwards. One mint per wallet per day.
